| 2026-09-22 | [[../slices/S-03-reservations-and-capacity-invariant\|S-03]] | An authenticated client reserves capacity for an invoice: `POST /programs/:programId/reservations` with `invoiceId`, `invoiceAmount` in minor units and `invoiceCurrency` answers `201` with the reservation (`reservedAmount`, `held`, `status` `active`, `source` `client`) and availability drops by the same amount. Refusals carry stable codes: `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the field, `404 PROGRAM_NOT_FOUND`, `409 RESERVATION_ALREADY_EXISTS` with the original reservation in the body, `422 CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` with `available`. Capacity is never overcommitted, proven with 25 parallel requests across two service instances on one database (a row lock per program, ADR-0008). When the treasury raises or lowers a limit, availability follows and a program below its usage reads `overcommitted` with every `held` kept. Every capacity movement is in an append-only ledger whose balances chain and are checked after every e2e test. A currency code is uppercased on both the HTTP and the Kafka edge, so `usd` means `USD` (A-10 amended). | AC-01, AC-02, AC-03, AC-04, AC-05, AC-08, AC-09, AC-21, AC-22, INV-01, INV-03, INV-04, INV-09, INV-11 | ADR-0008 (accepted before the slice); A-10 amended, glossary gains `source`, `Reservation identity`, `Currency code` | Same-currency reservations only: an invoice in another currency answers `422 CURRENCY_MISMATCH` until S-04 brings the client-supplied rate (then AC-07's `400` naming `rate`). No release and no read of one reservation yet (S-05); `held` equals `reservedAmount` for every reservation. `source` `reconciliation` is a legal state of the table but nothing produces it until S-06. The ledger check is a test helper, not a runtime guard. |
| 2026-09-23 | [[../slices/S-04-cross-currency-reservations\|S-04]] | A client reserves an invoice denominated in another currency than the program by sending `rate`, a decimal string of at most eight places: `POST /programs/:programId/reservations` converts once, rounds half up to the program's minor unit, stores the rate on the reservation and answers `201` with `reservedAmount`, `held` and the rate in canonical form (`"1.10"` reads back `"1.1"`). Each currency keeps the minor unit ISO 4217 gives it, so 1 000 JPY at `0.0067` reserves 6.70 USD and 1.00 USD at `0.30712` reserves 0.307 KWD, rather than both being treated as two decimals. A rate is required across two currencies and must be one within a currency; either mistake, and a rate that is not a positive decimal of at most eight places, answers `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` with `details` naming `rate`. The interim `422 CURRENCY_MISMATCH` on a reservation is gone: a reservation can no longer produce it. | AC-06, AC-07, INV-08 | ADR-0006 (accepted before the slice; its rate half applies here unchanged); A-10 amended twice, for the minor unit exponent and the canonical rate, glossary gains `Minor unit, minor unit exponent` | No release yet (S-05), so `held` still equals `reservedAmount` on every reservation and the stored rate is not converted back yet. The `reservations.rate` column has no `CHECK (rate > 0)`; nothing in the service can write such a row, since the mapper is the only writer, but the table's other constraint sets the pattern and this one is missing. The glossary still illustrates `Adapter`, `Seam` and `Fake` with a `RateProvider` and a config rate table that A-02 decided against, so the ubiquitous language teaches a seam the service does not contain. Run on Opus although the slice is `risk: high`, and both review rounds ran on Opus too because the Fable credits were exhausted, so the model independence `CLAUDE.md §8` buys was absent for this slice. |
| 2026-09-25 | [[../slices/S-05-releases\|S-05]] | A client releases a reservation, in full or in instalments, in invoice currency: `POST /programs/:programId/reservations/:invoiceId/releases` with `releaseId` and an optional `amount` and `reason` answers `200` with the reservation, `held` lowered by the converted amount and availability grown by the same. `held` is derived from what the invoice has left rather than decremented per release (ADR-0009), so instalments that do not divide evenly by the rate close at exactly zero: 1 000 000.00 EUR at 1.13 released as three thirds ends at `held` 0, not one minor unit off. Leaving `amount` out releases everything left. A repayment sent twice answers `409 RELEASE_ALREADY_PROCESSED` with what the first one did, so a retry is never a second repayment; `releaseId` is unique per reservation, so the same id on another invoice is a different release. `GET /programs/:programId/reservations/:invoiceId` returns the reservation with the movements that explain it, read at one instant so the movements always sum to the `held` beside them. A reservation is closed when the whole invoice has been released, which is not always when `held` reaches zero: a remainder worth less than half a minor unit of the program currency rounds `held` away while the invoice still owes, and such a reservation stays active and can be released to the end. | AC-10, AC-11, AC-12, AC-13, AC-14, AC-15, AC-16, AC-17, AC-18, AC-19, AC-34, INV-02 | ADR-0009 (accepted 2026-09-23, Option 2 and Option A); AC-15 and AC-27 amended, A-08 and A-09 amended, glossary gains `Released invoice amount` and loses the `RateProvider` illustrations | No reconciliation yet (S-06), so nothing produces an `adjustment` movement and `source` `reconciliation` remains a legal state nothing writes. ADR-0011 is still `proposed` and must be decided before S-06: it now also owes the rule that an adjustment dropping a reservation takes `releasedInvoiceAmount` to the whole invoice, which is what keeps AC-27's "`held` 0, status closed" true. The reservation read is not paginated, which no requirement asks for. Four review rounds found 39 findings, including two paths that answered `500` to ordinary client input and one regression a fix round introduced; every round, implementation and review alike, ran on Opus because the Fable credits were exhausted, so the model independence `CLAUDE.md §8` asks for never held for this slice. |
| 2026-09-25 | [[../slices/S-06-reconciliation-snapshots\|S-06]] | The treasury reconciles a program over Kafka with a snapshot, `type` `reconciliation_snapshot`: its full state as of `asOf`, the limit, the currency and every reservation it considers active with what each holds. The service compares it against that moment and never replaces wholesale: an invoice it did not know becomes a reservation with `source` `reconciliation`, one omitted is released by adjustment and closed, a different `held` is corrected, a closed one listed again is reopened, and every difference is an `adjustment` movement carrying the snapshot's `messageId`, visible on the reservation read. Nothing a client was told is undone: a reservation created after `asOf` is left alone, a client release after `asOf` stands, and a client reservation created within 30 s before `asOf` is kept when omitted (`RECONCILIATION_KEEP_WINDOW_SECONDS`). An older snapshot is recorded `stale`; shuffled and reversed delivery end in the same state as in-order delivery; replaying every message and request changes nothing. Availability reports the last snapshot's `asOf`. A message that fails to apply is tried three times, then dead-lettered with its error while consumption continues, and each failed attempt is kept in `treasury_message_failures`. `npm run dev:treasury -- snapshot` plays the treasury. | AC-26, AC-27, AC-28, AC-29, AC-30, AC-31, AC-42, AC-43, AC-44, INV-02 (as amended), INV-05, INV-06, INV-07 | ADR-0010 (Option 2, amended: no window on the treasury's clock), ADR-0011 (Option 1), ADR-0012 (1A, 2A, 3A), ADR-0013 (Option 4, three attempts, with a failures table); A-12 amended three times, A-13 twice, INV-02 and INV-07 amended, AC-42 to AC-44 added, glossary gains `Held correction`, `Keep window`, `Failed attempt`, `Reconciliation note` | Shipped over an open major, by Marcin's decision after three review rounds, as an edge case: a message nesting an unknown field about 5 000 arrays deep overflows the stack while parsing, which runs outside the three attempts, so it is redelivered forever and stalls its partition. Five minors from review round 3, carried: the last-movement read claims `DISTINCT ON` but Prisma removes duplicates in memory; a program with more active reservations than the 10 000 list bound is not covered by a bound test; the sum of movements after `asOf` is quadratic in the transaction; the glossary's `Snapshot moment` still says a reservation created at `asOf` is never changed; two doc comments are stacked in the ledger port. The list bound and the listed-total bound are local decisions of the slice, not yet written into A-11's message contract. Two snapshots of one moment with different content are outside INV-07 (A-13 clause 7). Implemented and reviewed on Opus, Fable being out of credits, so the model independence `CLAUDE.md §8` asks for was absent. |
